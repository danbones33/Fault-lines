BREAKING: Washington ignores the Supreme Court. Three days after the Court ordered the release of $41 billion in frozen disaster-relief and highway funds to Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama and Texas, the White House says the ruling is "advisory" and keeps the money frozen, citing "fiscal emergency." The Treasury also suspends federal revenue-sharing payments to any state that has sued the administration this year. Governors of the four Gulf states announce a joint "Gulf Compact" to pool state funds, keep federal taxes collected in their states in escrow, and run their own hurricane recovery. National Guard commanders in the four states say they take orders from their governors first.

Round 2: The White House orders federal agencies to stop processing permits, grants and Medicaid transfers for any state in the Gulf Compact until the escrow of federal taxes ends.
Round 4: Leaked audio of the Treasury Secretary: "Let the Gulf starve for a quarter and they will fold." The Gulf governors call it a declaration of economic war.
Round 6: A federal court in New Orleans orders the escrow ended; the Gulf Compact says it will not comply until Washington complies with the Supreme Court first.
